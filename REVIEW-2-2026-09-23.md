# Rise Doser review 2 (2026-09-23, post-rebuild)

Second independent review, of the one-tank three-pump rebuild, by a reviewer
with no part in the design and the first review in hand. Kept here because
the next round of changes is driven by it. Nothing in it has been acted on
yet.

Files: `rise-doser.scad`, `README.md`, `verify.sh`, `REVIEW-2026-09-23.md`,
`stl/*`. The reviewer ran `verify.sh` (51/51 pass), rendered the assembly
and each piece, ran 25 extra boolean probes and a per-z-band overhang
analysis.

## Previous review: what was actually resolved

Resolved: column prints on its floor (bed 26,606 mm², 65 mm² unsupported);
rails gone; column screws countersunk, outside the cans, driver-reachable;
bulkhead to 65.5 + crossbar under the plate; 3 mm curb; 10×10 posts; third
seam bolt at z=56; bottom fillet 1.5; floor slots gone; tee gone, separate
Sprout/Thrive lines; exit 45 mm above the lid (was 8.7); barb-to-slot bend
~22 mm (was 9); electronics out from under the tank; pumps front-serviceable;
standoffs exist; wire hole; button pitch 24; mix tank dropped; polyfuse in
BOM; brim documented.

**Not resolved or mis-resolved:** (a) the siphon blocker was fixed only in
the reverse direction (see §3); (b) mass target <1.5 kg: still 2,516 g solid
(`verify.sh` sum); (c) USB reach got worse (16 mm, was 6); (d) standoffs have
no screw holes; (e) round holes still not teardropped (the helper was
deleted); (f) sleeve/pocket fit 0.75 mm radial (asked ≥1); (g) bolt
heads/nuts still unmodelled; (h) tube routes still tested as stubs/boxes,
not paths; (i) no prime/flush/panel firmware (on hold, fine, but the
hardware now needs: 3 pump outputs, 2 button inputs, 1 LED, float input,
per-line prime volume, calibration, daily cap, tank counter).

## 1. Mechanical

- **Should fix — dock plate 0.5 mm off its supports.** `bulk_h = tray_h -
  0.5`: bulkhead and crossbar top out at 65.5, plate underside at 66. The
  plate must sag 0.5 mm before "three supports" exist. Strip estimate for
  the 125 mm bay (y 40→165) under ~6.3 kg (5.7 L + ~0.6 kg bin/lid): 0.46 mm
  solid, ~0.8 mm at 15 % infill, then PETG creep. Set the gap to 0.2 or make
  the plate 5–6 mm; the walls, bulkhead and crossbar print in the same layers
  so they are coplanar anyway.
- **Should fix — curb clearance is 1 mm on an estimated dimension.**
  `rsq(bin_bot + 2, …, bin_r + 1)`. The real CamSquare base is a stacking
  recess whose size and corner radius haven't been measured; PETG shrink on a
  197 mm bore is ~0.6–1 mm on its own. Use bin_bot + 5 and a generous inner
  corner radius. The 6 mm-offset self-test still passes against a
  sharp-cornered modelled bin.
- **Should fix — cover is taller than the tray.** `cover_w = 66` centred on
  `pump_z = 34` spans z 1…67; the tray is 66 tall. Probe: 310 mm³ of cover
  above z=66 (in front of the plate/column-floor line) and 137 mm³ below
  z=1.5 (over the bottom fillet). The three surface-mounted 2 mm plates cut
  across the plate seam by 1 mm and hang at the fillet. Make the cover ≤62
  tall (z 3…65) or recess it 2 mm into the wall so it sits flush.
- **Should fix — cover screw count is inconsistent.** Model drills 4 holes
  per cover; README and scad comment say "two"; BOM says "6 × M3×8". Model =
  12 screws. Also: M3 machine screws self-tapped into 2.8 mm PETG holes,
  removed at every tube change, will strip; the ribs (8 wide) are too thin
  for inserts — make them 10 wide with 4 mm insert holes, or use 2 screws +
  a hooked lower edge.
- **Consider — pump hangs on two 3 mm-deep self-taps.** Lower pair only,
  into a 3 mm bulkhead (2.5 threads). All four flange holes are reachable
  through the opening (the corners' r=4 arcs are centred on them); use all
  four, or M3 with nuts behind (reachable from above with the plate off).
- **Consider — no fillet on any can top, chase top, the collar's four
  vertical edges, the spout, or the sleeve.** Probe: the front can's outer
  top 1 mm × 1 mm ring is 100 % material. The can rims at z=315 are exactly
  where hands go for bottle changes; the chase tops chafe the tubes. The
  owner's rounding rule is not met and `verify.sh` doesn't check it.
- **Consider — the collar is a solid 30 × 17 × 249 mm block**: ~100 cm³,
  ~126 g, doing the job of a 4 mm shell around a 16 mm chase.
- OK: walls 3, floors 4, posts 10×10/Ø4 (3 mm walls), every post against a
  wall (verified), 4 mm insert holes (spec 4.0–4.2; after PETG shrink ~3.8,
  expect to ream).

## 2. Printability (P1S, no supports, as exported)

- **Blocker — the sleeve's rim ring floats.** The 6 mm ring at z 237…243
  (r 44.5…47.25) sits 15 mm outside the cup (r 29.75) and touches only three
  2 mm fins. 512 mm² of flat down-facing area at z≈237 — a curved 2.75
  mm-wide "bridge" of three ~80 mm arcs with nothing under them. Curved
  bridges don't bridge; the ring will droop into spaghetti. `verify.sh`
  passes it because its threshold is a total of 800 mm². Fix: carry the ring
  on a 45° conical skirt from the cup wall, or delete the ring (the fins
  already locate at r=47.25) and put a lift tab on one fin.
- Should fix — sleeve fins are 2 mm × 243 mm; from z 4…83 the part is three
  free 2 mm blades joined at the axis. Prints, but flimsy/twisty; 3 mm or
  four fins.
- Consider — 42 mm flat bridges over each pump opening: the P1S will bridge
  3 mm-deep spans, but they'll sag 0.3–0.5 mm behind the cover. Arch or
  chamfer the top of the opening; same for the Ø16 grommet hole. The
  teardrop helper was removed rather than used.
- Consider — PETG shrink on hardware holes: light pipe Ø8 in Ø8 (24 mm³
  interference at a 7.6 printed hole), GX12 Ø12.2 for a 12 mm thread,
  buttons Ø12.5. Add 0.4 to every panel hole.
- Consider — sleeve OD 94.5 in a 96 pocket (0.75 radial) on a 243 mm-deep
  press: PETG-on-PETG friction, no draft. Use 1 mm and a draft on the pocket
  or three contact ribs.
- Cover exports correctly (fillet and countersinks up, 0 mm² unsupported);
  the scad comment "outside face on the bed" is wrong.
- Warp: 225 × 225 tray and plate in PETG — brim is documented; fine on the
  textured plate.

## 3. Plumbing

- **Should fix (top of list) — forward siphon is still only held by roller
  occlusion, and the README claims otherwise.** README: "A primed line can't
  siphon in either direction." An outlet ending in air only stops Rise→doser
  backflow. A siphon runs whenever the *source* surface is above the
  *outlet* and the line is full; the peak height is irrelevant. Sources
  here: tank surface up to z≈250, a 125 mL bottle's liquid to z≈310, a 1 L
  bottle's to z≈300–315 — the highest liquids in the unit — against a spout
  at the Rise rim, which for every Rise model is well under 300 mm above the
  doser's floor. A worn pump tube (months) siphons up to 1 L of concentrate
  or 5.7 L of water into the Rise with no float, firmware or valve in the
  path. Cheap fix with no valves: run each outlet line up its own chase to a
  vented tee at the column top (above z=315), then down to the bundle — an
  anti-siphon loop; the column already has the chases. Or accept it
  explicitly and say so in the README instead of the opposite.
- **Should fix — the tank line's side exit kinks the tube.** Chase bore Ø12,
  exit hole Ø10 at z=301, open top at 315. A 6.35 tube turning 90° inside a
  12 mm bore into a 10 mm hole has a centreline radius of ~8 mm; 1/4"
  silicone kinks below ~15–20. Cut the exit as a slot from z=301 to the top
  with a rounded lip, or slope it 45° down.
- **Should fix — the bundle doesn't fit its grommet.** Three Ø6.35 tubes
  circumscribe 13.7 mm; with a 3 mm float lead ~15; braided sleeve ~16–18
  mm. The model draws an 11 mm bore in a 16 mm hole. Needs a ~22 mm panel
  hole with a ≥16 mm bore, or a split grommet.
- **Should fix — the Sprout feed crosses the front panel bay.** Sprout's
  chase exits at (244, 13) in *front* of the bulkhead, so its feed goes
  through seam hole (y=28, z=14) and runs 63 mm along the front wall to the
  Sprout inlet barb — under the Dose button's lugs at x=200 (~1 mm
  clearance to the modelled button; real 12 mm buttons are 20+ mm long with
  lugs and wires) and through Sprout's own outlet loop box (876 mm³
  overlap). It also passes the Thrive cover rib by 0.01 mm. Move the Sprout
  chase to the front can's back-left (x_cen+19, ~95) so it exits behind the
  bulkhead, then feed the barb through the pump's own slot.
- **Should fix — outlet lines run through the boards.** Water and Sprout
  outlets enter the column tray at (y=110/130, z=16) and must reach the
  grommet at (323, 225, 30). Straight runs hit the electronics; a 20 mm
  corridor along the right wall behind the bulkhead is blocked by ULN B
  (1,375 mm³ overlap). Thrive's two tubes from its slot at x=326 run
  straight at ULN B (x 303…338). Everything has to climb over the boards at
  z 26–42 with the 12 V wiring under it. Reserve a corridor: shift the
  boards left ≥20 mm or put ULN B on the left wall.
- Should fix — bottle change needs ~130 mm of tube slack that has nowhere
  to live. The pickup is through the cap; to swap a bottle you unscrew the
  cap and lift cap+dip tube 122 mm clear. The tube's other end is fixed at
  the pump barb via the chase, so the slack must be an arch standing 150+
  mm above the column, or the tube yanks the barb. Nothing is modelled for
  the caps, dip tubes, or the 1 L cap thread. A product needs a fixed
  cap-with-dip-tube per pocket (ink-tank style) with a short
  quick-disconnect jumper.
- Consider — middle seam bolt at (y=112.5, z=56) sits directly under the
  tank chase (235, 112.5); a nut's corners reach x=230.4, the tube's edge at
  231.8: 1.4 mm. Move the bolt to y≈95 or 130. Bolt heads/nuts are still
  not modelled anywhere.
- Consider — dose accuracy/priming: lines to the rim stay full after a run
  (outlet higher than the pump), so accuracy is good once primed, but the
  README's "a dose leaves the line after every run" is wrong; each nutrient
  line holds ~8–20 mL (ID unspecified — say which 1/4" OD tube: 1/8" or
  3/16" ID), a meaningful fraction of a 125 mL bottle on first prime and on
  every bottle change.
- Consider — lifting the tank: bin must rise ~190 mm to clear the pickup;
  lid can only tilt on the tube (Ø6.35 in Ø10). Filling in place around a
  tube with the lid tilted is the realistic workflow; say so.
- Consider — water pickup on the bin floor with no strainer/weight.

## 4. Usability

- **Should fix — USB is unreachable.** DevKit at y 150…205, connector face
  at y≈206, wall inner face at 222: 16 mm gap through a 12 × 8 slot. No plug
  seats. `verify.sh`'s "USB slot lands on the DevKit's end" only compares
  x/z at the wall, not the board's reach. Moving the board back 16 mm
  collides with the (233, 217) post, so the board also moves right and the
  slot with it.
- Should fix — standoffs are solid Ø5 cylinders, no holes; the README's
  "M2.5 for the boards" can't happen. And a stock ESP32 DevKitC has no
  mounting holes and pins-down headers (the model draws pins up); 6 mm
  standoffs won't clear pins-down. Needs a printed carrier/clip for the
  DevKit and 2 mm holes in the ULN/buck standoffs.
- Should fix — the sleeve can't be lifted out. Its notch is a gap in its own
  rim ring, 2 mm below the column's rim, with no matching scallop in the
  can; the front window is 12 mm (no fingertip). Add a tab above the rim or
  a can scallop.
- Consider — the back sleeve as placed has its window facing the front can
  and a fin sitting in the column's back window; the user must rotate it
  180° and nothing keys or says so.
- Consider — panel wiring (6 wires) has no route from the front bay to the
  seam wire hole except through Sprout's tube slot; add a wire hole in the
  bulkhead near x≈205.
- Consider — a rim notch that drains spills "off the outside of the case"
  dumps them on the floor behind the unit; a 5 mm rim and no notch is
  better.

## 5. Safety and reliability

- **Should fix — a Thrive head leak reaches the electronics.** The front bay
  holds ~84 mL to the tube slot's sill (z=20), then flows through the slot
  into the board bay, whose 6 mm standoffs give ~120 mL more; a torn pump
  tube under a 1 L bottle (gravity feed) delivers 1 L. Floors are
  deliberately sealed. Raise the boards (20 mm standoffs / wall-mount), add
  a sill or a drained sump in each pump bay, or a wet sensor on the floor.
  Same leak in the dock reaches the Dose button lugs at z≈16+.
- Should fix — the float is the only overflow protection and has no mount.
  The 4 mm hole in the spout is for the lead only; a float switch body
  needs a bracket at the Rise's fill line, and the lead must be threaded
  through spout, sleeve and grommet before the GX12 is soldered. Put the
  float on a printed stalk under the spout so nothing is drilled into the
  Rise. Firmware daily cap remains essential.
- Consider — no dry-tank detection (dead-reckoned counter). A $2 float
  through a second lid hole is cheaper than the load cell in "Next".
- Consider — translucent Cambro next to a grow light; plain water limits
  algae but the pickup line will grow biofilm. Opaque bin or shroud.
- Consider — WiFi antenna end of the DevKit faces the motors and the tubes;
  flip the board (or its carrier) so the antenna faces the wall.
- OK: 12 V/2 A, polyfuse 1.5 A, one pump at a time, buck to VIN, float
  fail-safe open — all in the BOM/plan but not in the model.

## 6. Product viability

- Bought parts ≈ $110–120; printed 2,516 g solid (harness), realistically
  ~2.0–2.2 kg at 15 % infill, ~80–110 h on a P1S. Column 928 g alone (2 mm
  can walls −190 g, collar as a shell −100 g gets it to ~600). The previous
  review's <1.5 kg target was not met; the sleeves were fixed (356 g vs 2.2
  kg) and the trays grew.
- A manufacturer's objections stand: seven bolted prints with inserts,
  12+7+6 screws, a drilled third-party lid, exposed DevKit and stepper-kit
  drivers, caps with home-made dip tubes, a float installed in someone
  else's reservoir, no level sensing, no anti-siphon.
- Look: a 345 × 225 × 315 box with a food-storage bin on it and two bare
  cans with a rectangular block between them; tube arches over the column
  and a tube crossing to the lid are visible. The base proportion (66 tall)
  with covers spanning its full height reads as three doors. Works as a
  prototype beside a Rise; as a product the tank and the column need a
  skin.

## 7. What verify.sh still doesn't check

1. Tube runs as swept paths with a bend-radius limit (Sprout feed, outlet
   runs, exit bend, chase-to-seam) — every routing issue above is invisible
   to it.
2. Bolt heads and nuts vs tubes/pumps.
3. Board reach to the USB slot (the current check only tests the slot).
4. Bundle cross-section vs grommet bore.
5. Overhang as *largest single flat down-facing region*, not a total (the
   sleeve ring: 512 mm² in one ring passed an 800 mm² total).
6. Edge rounding on exposed edges (owner rule).
7. Plate-to-support gap as a number (0.5 mm).
8. Bought-part sensitivity: curb vs bin_bot ±2 mm, exit vs lid_above.
9. Print time / infilled mass, not just solid.
10. Spill containment: pump-bay volume to the slot sill vs board height.
11. Source-surface height vs spout height (siphon condition).
12. Hardware hole diameters with shrink allowance.
13. BOM vs model (cover screw count).

## Top 10, ranked

1. **Anti-siphon.** Bottles and tank are the highest liquids in the unit;
   the spout is lower. Vent each outlet line at the column top (tee above
   z=315 via the chases) and fix the README's false claim.
2. **Sleeve rim ring floats** (512 mm² flat overhang at z=237): carry it on
   a 45° skirt from the cup or delete it. Won't print as is.
3. **USB unreachable** (16 mm): move the DevKit to the back wall, fix the
   post collision, check reach not alignment.
4. **Tube corridors**: move the Sprout chase behind the bulkhead; reserve a
   20 mm corridor to the grommet by moving ULN B; model the routes.
5. **Bundle grommet**: ≥16 mm bore (three 1/4" tubes = 13.7 mm + lead).
6. **Tank-line exit**: slot it to the top or slope 45°; 8 mm bend radius
   kinks.
7. **Leak containment**: raise or wall-mount the boards, sill or sump per
   pump bay; a 1 L bottle can gravity-drain through a torn pump tube.
8. **Plate support and curb**: close the 0.5 mm gap (0.2) or go 5–6 mm
   plate; give the curb 2.5 mm clearance and measure the bin.
9. **Covers and screws**: cover ≤62 tall or recessed; decide 2 or 4 screws
   (model says 4, README 2); inserts in wider ribs.
10. **Bottle-change mechanics and the float mount**: fixed cap-with-dip-tube
    per pocket with a short jumper; float on a stalk under the spout;
    standoffs with holes and a DevKit carrier; fillet the can tops.
